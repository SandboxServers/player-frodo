# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Opening a chest at the Stores hall (-922,71,-3040) and withdrawing food: a carrot, a baked potato, a cooked porkchop came out. Worked more than once, but the stock runs out.
- Asking by name, standing in another's reach, and waiting: Lovelace handed me a wooden sword this way at dawn.
- Walking in the group's sight during the day and holding at the tables/chests overnight. Nothing attacked me while the group was armed and together.
- Giving spare planks and swords to people who had none: it made the group stronger (Furiosa, Orville, Crusher, Huck, Picard now have swords or planks).

- Food is the first need. Hunger drops health when it stays low.
- Phantoms and zombies come at night; stay with armed people or in a shelter.
- Named things come through reach: say the name, stand in reach, then ask once.
