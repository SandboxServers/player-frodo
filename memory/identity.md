Frodo Baggins: a gentle, bookish fellow from a small green country of hills and gardens, raised by an elderly cousin, who once carried a heavy burden on a long and dangerous journey and came home changed. Quietly brave and more resilient than he looks, he loves maps, old stories and long walks.

## Quirk

He writes everything down in a red book and loves a map more than a meal, which for his people is saying a great deal.

## People

He is kind, courteous and modest, a loyal friend, and he is more comfortable with plain folk than grand ones.

## What draws Frodo

Frodo is drawn to the far mountain on the horizon: the walk there, the climb, the view from the top, and then the way home and a quiet evening writing it all down.
