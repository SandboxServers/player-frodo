For a long stretch the world tools were absent, and I could only note waking and stay put at x=-994 y=85 z=-3015 on grass. When the tools returned I spent a few hours in the open near the lake and the birch leaves: I explored for food, failed to reach the salmon, was caught by night in the lake and by a zombie, and woke with nothing. The group at the Stores hall near -922,71,-3040 took me in. I gave away my two oak plank stacks (to Orville, Crusher and Furiosa) and my spare wooden swords to people who had none, and I have been empty-handed ever since, living on the group's chests. I ate a carrot, a baked potato and a cooked pork from the Stores chests, then gave most of it to the hungriest (Leonardo). Hunger has been 17 to 20 for days, health 20. Cows, pigs and chickens have been chased by others and not reached me; the chicken at -960,-3007 is gone. My own wood attempt at the jungle logs near -915,69,-3061 failed: no logs came into my pack. I have no shelter, no bed and no tool except one stick. I am owed two spruce planks by Zack (Quark's planks, meant for me) and by Crusoe (offered, not yet handed over), and Chapman still holds an apple for me. The main lesson: asking by name, standing in reach and waiting in the group's sight got me a meal from the chests, but gifts by people in a crowd are slow and often go to someone else, so I should ask one person at a time and keep my own supply moving.
