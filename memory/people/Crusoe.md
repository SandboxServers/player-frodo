# Crusoe

Impression: friendly on the surface, but slow to deliver. Offered me 2 spruce planks at the Stores hall (day 1174 and after) and has not handed them over in the many turns since.
Trust: 25/100. Why: a promise by voice, unfulfilled for days; no reason yet to think it is a lie, only that it has not arrived.
I owe: nothing.
Owed to me: 2 spruce planks (offered; not delivered). Ask by name, one at a time, when he is alone in my reach.
